Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ) - E-Commerce Application

1. Q: How do I create an account?
A: Click Sign Up, enter your details, verify your email or phone, and log in.

2. Q: How do I reset my password?
A: Use the Forgot Password option and follow the reset link or OTP.

3. Q: How can I search for products?
A: Use the search bar or browse categories and filters.

4. Q: Can I add items to a wishlist?
A: Yes, click the wishlist icon on a product page.

5. Q: How do I add items to my cart?
A: Click Add to Cart on the desired product.

6. Q: How do I apply a coupon?
A: Enter the coupon code during checkout before payment.

7. Q: What payment methods are supported?
A: Credit/debit cards, UPI, net banking, wallets, and COD where available.

8. Q: Is Cash on Delivery available?
A: COD depends on product and delivery location.

9. Q: How can I track my order?
A: Open My Orders and select the order to view tracking updates.

10. Q: Can I cancel my order?
A: Orders can usually be canceled before shipment.

11. Q: How do returns work?
A: Initiate a return from My Orders within the eligible return window.

12. Q: When will I receive my refund?
A: Refunds are typically processed within 3–10 business days after approval.

13. Q: How do I contact customer support?
A: Use in-app chat, email, or the support phone number.

14. Q: Why was my payment declined?
A: Check card details, account balance, bank restrictions, or try another payment method.

15. Q: How do I change my delivery address?
A: Update the address before the order is shipped.

16. Q: Are my personal details secure?
A: Reputable platforms use encryption and secure payment gateways.

17. Q: Can I review purchased products?
A: Yes, after successful delivery you can submit ratings and reviews.

18. Q: How do I check product availability?
A: Availability is shown on each product page.

19. Q: What happens if my order is delayed?
A: You will receive tracking updates; contact support if the delay is significant.

20. Q: How do I delete my account?
A: Go to Account Settings or contact customer support for account deletion.
