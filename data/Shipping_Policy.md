# Global Shipping & Delivery Policy

**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026  
**Last Updated:** February 1, 2026  
**Document ID:** POL-SHP-2026-V2

---

## 1. Overview & Commitment
This Shipping and Delivery Policy outlines the terms, timelines, methods, and procedures governing the shipment of products purchased through our enterprise e-commerce platform. We partner with premier logistics providers—including FedEx, UPS, USPS, and DHL Express—to ensure reliable, timely, and secure delivery of your orders worldwide.

---

## 2. Order Processing & Fulfillment Timelines

### 2.1 Standard Processing Window
* All standard in-stock orders are processed and packaged within **1 to 2 business days** (Monday through Friday, excluding federal and state holidays).
* Orders placed on Saturday, Sunday, or public holidays will begin processing on the following business day.

### 2.2 Same-Day Dispatch Cutoff
* Orders placed with **Expedited** or **Overnight Express** shipping before **2:00 PM Eastern Standard Time (EST)** on business days are dispatched from our fulfillment center the same day.
* Orders placed after 2:00 PM EST will be dispatched the next business day.

### 2.3 Order Verification & Fraud Screening
* High-value orders or transactions requiring manual security verification may experience an additional 24-hour processing delay while billing details are validated.

## 4. Free Shipping Policy & Exclusions

* **Eligibility Threshold:** Free Standard Ground Shipping applies automatically at checkout to domestic US orders totaling **$50.00 or higher** prior to taxes and after any promotional discount codes are applied.
* **Exclusions:** Heavy items (exceeding 50 lbs / 22.7 kg) or oversized freight items are excluded from free ground shipping and are subject to calculated freight delivery surcharges displayed at checkout.

---

## 5. Non-Contiguous US, PO Boxes, & Military Addresses (APO/FPO/DPO)

### 5.1 Alaska, Hawaii, and US Territories
* Shipments to Alaska, Hawaii, Puerto Rico, Guam, and the US Virgin Islands ship via USPS Priority Mail or FedEx 2-Day Air.
* Standard transit time is **5 to 9 business days**. A $9.99 remote location surcharge applies.

### 5.2 PO Box Deliveries
* Shipments to PO Boxes must be fulfilled exclusively via **USPS**. FedEx and UPS private couriers cannot deliver to federal PO Box receptacles.

### 5.3 Military Addresses (APO/FPO/DPO)
* We proudly support military personnel. All APO, FPO, and DPO shipments are routed via USPS Priority Mail.
* Estimated transit times vary between **10 to 21 business days** depending on overseas military postal operating conditions.

---

## 6. International Shipping & Customs

### 6.1 Supported Destinations
We ship to over 65 countries worldwide, including Canada, the United Kingdom, European Union member states, Australia, New Zealand, Japan, and Singapore.

### 6.2 International Transit Timelines
* **Standard International (DHL eCommerce / Postal):** 7 to 14 business days.
* **Express International (DHL Express / FedEx International Priority):** 2 to 5 business days.

### 6.3 Customs, Duties, and Import Taxes (DDP vs. DDU)
* **Delivered Duty Paid (DDP):** For supported destinations (UK, EU, Canada, Australia), all applicable import duties, customs tariffs, and VAT/GST are calculated and collected directly at checkout. No surprise fees will be charged upon delivery.
* **Delivered Duty Unpaid (DDU):** For countries not supporting DDP, the recipient is responsible for all local customs clearance fees, import tariffs, and duties levied by local customs authorities.

## 7. Real-Time Tracking & Shipment Notifications

* **Tracking Number Issuance:** As soon as your order is packaged and a carrier shipping label is generated, an automated confirmation email and SMS notification containing your tracking number and carrier tracking link are sent.
* **Tracking Scan Updates:** Please allow up to **24 hours** after label generation for the carrier to perform the initial origin facility scan and display active transit checkpoints.
* **In-App Tracking:** You can view real-time package location and delivery progress anytime in the **My Orders** portal.

---

## 8. Signature Requirements & High-Value Packages

* **Mandatory Signature on Delivery:** Orders valued at **$500.00 USD or greater**, as well as high-end consumer electronics and fine jewelry, automatically require a direct adult signature upon delivery to prevent theft.
* **Carrier Redelivery:** If no adult is present to sign, the carrier will attempt up to **3 consecutive delivery attempts** before holding the parcel at a local pickup facility for 5 business days.

---

## 9. Address Changes, Order Intercepts & Delivery Edits

* **Prior to Dispatch:** You may update your shipping address without charge through your account dashboard or by contacting Customer Support within **1 hour** of placing the order.
* **Post-Dispatch Intercepts:** Once a shipment is in transit with FedEx or UPS, a carrier package redirect or delivery intercept can be requested for a **$15.00 carrier administrative fee**, subject to carrier feasibility.
* **Undeliverable as Addressed / Returned to Sender (RTS):** Packages returned due to incorrect, incomplete, or vacated addresses will be refunded minus the original outbound shipping cost and a $5.00 return handling fee.

---

## 10. Damaged, Lost, or Stolen Packages

### 10.1 Packages Damaged in Transit
* If your parcel arrives visibly damaged, please photograph the external box, shipping label, and damaged contents immediately.
* Claims for damaged items must be submitted to Customer Support within **48 hours of carrier delivery timestamp**.

### 10.2 Lost Shipments (In-Transit Inactivity)
* If your tracking status shows no carrier scan updates for more than **7 consecutive business days** (domestic) or **14 business days** (international), our team will initiate a formal carrier trace and issue a free replacement or full refund.

### 10.3 Stolen Packages / Porch Piracy
* If a package is marked as "Delivered" by the carrier but cannot be located, please check building entrances, parcel lockers, and with neighbors.
* If still missing after 24 hours, contact Customer Support within **5 days of delivery confirmation** to initiate a security investigation and claim resolution.

---

## 11. Hazardous Materials & Transportation Restrictions (HAZMAT)

* Certain items containing lithium-ion batteries, pressurized aerosols, alcohol-based perfumes, or chemical solvents are classified as Hazardous Materials by the US Department of Transportation (DOT) and IATA.
* HAZMAT items must ship via **Standard Surface Ground Transportation** only and cannot be shipped via Air, Priority Overnight, or to APO/FPO addresses.

---

## 12. Holiday Peak Shipping Deadlines

During the peak November–December holiday season, carrier networks experience high volumes. To ensure delivery by December 24th:
* **Standard Ground:** Order on or before **December 15th, 11:59 PM EST**.
* **Expedited 2-Day:** Order on or before **December 20th, 2:00 PM EST**.
* **Priority Overnight:** Order on or before **December 22nd, 2:00 PM EST**.

## 13. Sustainability & Consolidated Eco-Packaging

* **Consolidated Shipping:** By default, multi-item orders are consolidated into a single shipment to minimize carbon footprint and packaging waste.
* **Eco-Packaging:** All outer shipping boxes and packing materials are manufactured from 100% recycled paperboard and are fully biodegradable.
* **Carbon-Neutral Shipping Option:** Customers can opt-in at checkout to offset shipment emissions via our certified reforestation partners.
